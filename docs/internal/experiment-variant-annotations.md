# Experiment variant annotations

Use the **Variant annotations** panel on an experiment's Metrics tab to record notes about individual variants.

Variants with annotations show a comment icon and count beside their name in the metric results table, including breakdown rows. Click the indicator to read the three latest annotations for that variant, with their authors and creation times. Select **View all annotations** to scroll to and focus the panel.

Adding, moving, or deleting an annotation updates the indicators from the same shared list used by the panel. Variants without annotations keep their usual labels.
